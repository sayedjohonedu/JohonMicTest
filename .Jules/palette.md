## 2026-07-05 - Modal Close Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only close buttons (like '&times;' or SVGs) in custom popups and modals within this app (often using the class 'modal-close-btn') frequently lack accessibility attributes.
**Action:** Always verify that such buttons include 'aria-label="Close"' and 'title="Close"' to ensure keyboard and screen reader accessibility.
