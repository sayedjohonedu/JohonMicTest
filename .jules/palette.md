## 2026-07-06 - App-Specific Close Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only close buttons (e.g., `&times;` or SVG) in custom popups and modals (often identifiable by classes like `modal-close-btn` or `btn-close`) frequently lack accessibility attributes.
**Action:** Always verify and ensure these elements include `aria-label="Close"` and `title="Close"`.
